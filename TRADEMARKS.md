# Trade marks

The AGPL-3.0 licenses the **code**. It does not license the **name or the
marks**, and this file exists so the boundary is explicit rather than assumed.

"Actualis", the Actualis A-mark, and the Actualis visual identity are trade
marks of Digital Foundry Solutions, LLC.

## What you may do

- Use the name to refer to this project — in prose, docs, a package listing, a
  blog post, a talk, a comparison table.
- State accurately that your software uses, integrates with, or is built on
  Actualis.
- Fork the code. The AGPL guarantees this and nothing here narrows it.

## What you may not do

- Name or brand a fork "Actualis", or a name likely to be confused with it.
  Fork the code freely; give it your own name.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests endorsement, affiliation, or that your
  distribution is the official one.
- Use the marks as, or as part of, your own product name, company name, domain
  name, or app-store listing.

## The brand artwork

The Actualis brand sheets and icon sheets are **not** included in this
repository, deliberately. Shipping them under the AGPL would invite forks to
ship the identity along with the code, which is exactly what the marks exist to
prevent.

The tray icon geometry is nonetheless fully reproducible: every ratio is
recorded, with its provenance, in `tray-go/icons/gen_icons.py`, and the reasons
for each deviation are in `docs/decisions/0001-tray-icon-presence.md`. A fork
can regenerate equivalent iconography and should then re-colour and re-name it
as its own.
